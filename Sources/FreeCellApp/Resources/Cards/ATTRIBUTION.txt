Changes by Fungleo: rasterized SVG artwork to 528 x 768 PNG; mapped C/D/H/S, A through K to game IDs 1 through 52. The original aspect ratio differs by less than 0.2% from the game's ratio.
Full upstream README and the 52 original SVGs are preserved in App/CardSources in the source repository.

Approved styling: warm ivory radial-gradient paper with a subtle amber wash; bold inset corner indices and redrawn corner suit symbols; central artwork scaled uniformly (A 90%, 2-10 88%, J/Q/K 82%, shifted upward for stacked head visibility). White illustration regions and embedded artwork backgrounds were tinted ivory. Original upstream SVGs are preserved; styled SVGs are in App/CardSources/Adjusted.

Stacking revision: face artwork enlarged and raised; corner clubs redrawn as three rounded lobes with a stem; paper and white regions slightly darkened.

Portrait revision: J/Q/K artwork now centered at 110% of upstream size; removed only the rounded portrait outline and redundant suit ornaments to keep corner indices clear. Internal illustration linework is preserved.
